PDF to Word

No file yet
📄 Drop a PDF here

or click to browse — produces an editable .docx

Now rebuilt for fidelity: fonts, sizes, bold/italic, text colours, alignment, headings, tables and page margins are reconstructed from the PDF's own text layer, and everything lands in Word as real editable text and tables — not a picture of the page. Faithful for text-based PDFs (typed documents, exports, reports). Scanned pages have no text layer; for those, the OCR button below reads the page images with Tesseract — best-effort only, and layout is not preserved there. Complex artwork, logos and exact pixel positioning are not carried over.

PDF to Excel

No file yet
📄 Drop a PDF here

or click to browse — produces an .xlsx with one sheet per page

Now rebuilt for fidelity: columns and rows are reconstructed from where each piece of text actually sits on the page, numbers are stored as real Excel numbers, wrapped lines are merged back into their cell, the header row is bolded (with its original shading colour when the PDF shows one), column widths follow the PDF and the header row is frozen. Faithful for text-based PDFs with a real text layer. Scanned pages have no text layer; the OCR button reads them with Tesseract, which is best-effort and approximate — always check figures.

Compress PDF

No file yet
📄 Drop a PDF here

or click to browse — make the file smaller for email & sharing

About 120 DPI, JPEG quality 70% — a good balance for sharing and email.

How it works: each page is re-rendered as an image at the level you pick, then rebuilt into a new PDF. That means text in the compressed file is no longer selectable or searchable, and a small, already-optimised PDF can occasionally come out larger — you'll see the exact before & after sizes with your result. Compression keeps running even if you switch tabs or minimise the window, so you can safely do other work while a big file finishes.

The preview shows one page at screen quality — the downloaded file is rendered at the full output quality picked above.

How it works: every page is rendered and cleaned up pixel by pixel — the same treatment whether the PDF started life as a typed document or a scan. Clean scan mode estimates the paper colour behind the text and divides it out, so grey backgrounds, shadows and show-through fade to white while the text stays dark. Like Compress, the enhanced copy rebuilds pages as high-quality images, so its text is no longer selectable or searchable — keep the original alongside it.
